Callaway

1. Advanced technology is a major focus. Callaway uses AI-driven design and advanced materials in its clubs to improve distance, accuracy and forgiveness.

2. They make equipment for many skill levels. Callaway offers different club families designed for different types of golfers, from beginners to advanced players.

3. Odyssey gives Callaway a major advantage in putters. Odyssey has been one of the leading putter brands and has produced popular designs such as the 2-Ball and White Hot.

4. Callaway offers a complete golf lineup. The company produces drivers, irons, fairway woods, hybrids, wedges, putters and golf balls, along with other golf equipment.

5. It is an established global golf company. Callaway was founded in 1982 and has grown into a global company with brands including Callaway, Odyssey, OGIO and TravisMathew.
