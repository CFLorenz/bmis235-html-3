Titleist

1. Pro V1 is one of the most trusted golf balls in the world. Titleist says the Pro V1 has been the best-selling golf ball globally since its introduction in 2000.

2. Strongest PGA Tour presence. Titleist was the most-played golf-ball brand on the PGA Tour, with Pro V1/Pro V1x accounting for about 70% of player usage in 2024.

3. Titleist is strong across more than just golf balls. Its drivers, irons, wedges, hybrids and putters are also heavily used by professionals.

4. Vokey wedges are extremely popular. Vokey has been the most-played wedge on the PGA Tour since 2004.

5. Major focus on research and quality. Titleist has used more than 125 patents in different generations of Pro V1/Pro V1x and performs extensive quality testing on its golf balls.
